Child Safety Standards – Inside Me

Inside Me strictly prohibits any content related to child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAM).

We actively moderate user-generated content and provide in-app reporting tools to allow users to report inappropriate or illegal material.

Any detected CSAM content will be removed immediately and reported to relevant legal authorities in accordance with applicable laws.

Users under 13 years of age are not permitted to use this application.
